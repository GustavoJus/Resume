Barbershop Website

Description

I collaborated with my group to design and develop a barbershop website, where we worked together on the layout, content, and functionality to create a clean, user-friendly site that showcases services, pricing, and booking information.

  • Learn the use the WIX website
  • Organizing and presenting content clearly
  • Problem-solving and debugging as a group
  • Time management and meeting project deadlines
