Google TPU Research

Description

I collaborated with my group on a research project about Google TPUs, covering their introduction, history, technical aspects, social and professional impact, and ethical and legal issues. We summarized our findings into a structured presentation and concluded by engaging the audience through a Q&A session.

  • Technical research and analysis
  • Understanding specialized hardware (Google TPUs)
  • Organizing complex topics into a clear structure
  • Presentation and public speaking skills
  • Teamwork and collaborative problem-solving
  • Critical thinking about social, ethical, and legal impacts
